Choosing Your First Event

New to the SCA, or just new to the area? Welcome. Going to your first event is easier than it looks — here's how to pick a good one and what to expect.

Start with something local and low-key

You don't have to begin with a week-long war. The gentlest first outings are usually:

  • A local practice or meeting — fighter practice, an arts & sciences (A&S) night, or a populace meeting. Casual, free or cheap, and a great place to meet your local group.
  • A one-day event — a tournament, an A&S day, or a feast. A full day of the Current Middle Ages with a clear start and end.

Big multi-day camping events and wars are wonderful, but they're an easier leap once you've been to a day event or two and know a few people.

What to expect

  • You don't need anything to start. Many groups keep loaner "garb" (medieval clothing) on hand — look for events that mention being newcomer-friendly or having a Gold Key (loaner garb). Comfortable clothes that vaguely avoid obvious modern logos are plenty for a first visit.
  • People are happy to help. Tell someone it's your first event; most groups have a chatelaine or hospitaller whose whole job is welcoming newcomers.
  • Bring a few basics. Water, a snack, cash for the site fee, and a chair are rarely wrong.

Using this map to find one

  1. Pan and zoom to your area, or use the date filter to see what's coming up.
  2. Click a pin to see the event's name, date, and location.
  3. Follow the event's link to the official listing for the full details — fees, schedule, what to bring, and a contact for questions.

Always confirm the details on that official listing before you go. This map is an unofficial finding aid (see About This Project); the kingdom's own event page is the authority on dates, sites, and fees.
